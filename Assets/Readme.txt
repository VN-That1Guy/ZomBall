Welcome to the Zomball prototype!

There is no health or live system yet (neither a scoring one that's in use and a shopping system)
but what I hope to accomplish for this prototype is the general gameplay loop of the game when you have 
a gun and ammo to defend yourself while you are trying to play the pinball machine!

Features:
	Destructible arms and head for zombies!
		- Will wonder around aimlessly if it survives a decapitation
		- Flashes Red from damage
	Zombie Spawners
	A gun with these mechanics:
		- Spread
		- Damage
		- Damage Properties such as: Weak point multipliers and Damage Over Time
		- Aim "Lag" (In Visuals, not in Gameplay)
	A basic working Pinball Machine with Bumpers, Flippers and a Kinematic Rigid Body Plunger

Controls:
	General
	- Turn Around - Hover Cursor over to switch view from the pinball machine to the barricade with zombies 
			behind you

	Pinball
	- A and D key - Left and Right flipper
	- Space (Hold) - Pull the plunger
	- Space (Release) - Let go of the plunger (Can launch the pinball)

	Gun mode (Defending against the zombies behind you)
	- Mouse Cursor - Weapon Aiming direction
	- Left Click - Shoot
